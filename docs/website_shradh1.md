SHRADH
Some bonds don't end. They become part of who we are.
We inherit many things from our families.
A name.
A home.
Stories.
Values.
A way of looking at life.
And sometimes, traditions we may not fully understand anymore.
Shradh is one of them.
In the Sanatan tradition, Shradh is a way of remembering and honouring those who came before us — with श्रद्धा, gratitude and remembrance.
Not because we are afraid.
Because we remember.
And in today's world, where families can be spread across cities, countries and continents, Dharmaa Tribe brings this ancient practice into modern family life — without losing its meaning.
________________________________________
So, what exactly is Shradh?
Shradh is a traditional ancestral observance performed to honour departed family members.
It may involve Sankalp, Tarpan, Pind Daan, Mantra, Prarthana, Daan, Bhojan, Homa and other prescribed practices, depending on the family tradition and the nature of the ritual.
It is commonly observed during Pitru Paksha, and can also be performed annually on the relevant lunar Tithi associated with a loved one's passing.
But here's something important:
There isn't one Shradh that fits every family.
Your region may have its own practice.
Your family may follow a particular tradition.
Your Vedacharya may prescribe a specific Vidhi.
At Dharmaa, we begin there.
Not with a package.
With understanding.
________________________________________
Why do we perform Shradh?
Perhaps the simplest answer is:
Because we didn't get here alone.
Our lives carry pieces of those who came before us.
Their choices.
Their sacrifices.
Their values.
Their stories.
Their संस्कार.
Shradh gives us a traditional way to pause and acknowledge that connection.
It is an act of remembrance, gratitude and responsibility towards our lineage.
Shradh is not about fear.
It is about श्रद्धा.
________________________________________
When should Shradh be performed?
This is where things can get confusing.
Pitru Paksha? Annual Shradh? Which Tithi? Which ritual? Which place?
And if you're living abroad, the questions can multiply.
Shradh is generally performed according to the lunar Tithi associated with the ancestor, rather than simply the date on the English calendar.
It may be observed:
During Pitru Paksha
A period traditionally dedicated to remembering and honouring ancestors.
As Varshik Shradh
An annual observance on the relevant Tithi.
At a Sacred Tirtha
Certain ancestral rites are traditionally associated with places such as Gaya, Varanasi, Haridwar, Prayagraj, Gokarna, Pushkar and other sacred locations.
Don't know the Tithi?
Don't know the Gotra?
Not sure which ritual is appropriate?
That's okay.
You don't need to know everything before you begin.
That's what a Vedacharya is for.
________________________________________
What actually happens during Shradh?
The exact Vidhi varies according to tradition.
But depending on the ritual, you may encounter:
Sankalp
Putting your intention into words — who you are performing the ritual for and why.
Tarpan
Traditional offerings of water, often with sesame and prescribed mantras.
Pind Daan
Offering Pindas as prescribed in the particular ancestral ritual.
Mantra & Prarthana
Sacred recitation and prayer.
Homa / Havan
Where prescribed.
Daan & Bhojan
Traditional offerings, charity or feeding, as prescribed.
And finally—
Remembering.
Because sometimes the most important part of a ritual is not what we offer...
but whom we remember while offering it.

Pind Daan is a traditional ancestral offering that forms an important part of several Shradh and Tirtha rituals.
It is particularly associated with Gaya Ji, where ancestral rites have a deep and longstanding pilgrimage tradition.
The exact practice and ingredients can vary according to the ritual and tradition being followed.

It forms an important part of many ancestral observances.

### Question: How does Dharmaa Tribe conduct Shradh?

**Answer:**

At Dharmaa Tribe, we believe Shradh should be performed in a way that is **authentic to the family’s tradition, practical for the family, and guided by knowledgeable Vedacharyas.**

### प्रश्न: Dharmaa Tribe श्राद्ध कैसे संपन्न कराता है?

**उत्तर:**

Dharmaa Tribe का मानना है कि श्राद्ध ऐसी विधि से संपन्न होना चाहिए जो **परिवार की परंपरा के अनुरूप हो, परिवार के लिए सहज और व्यावहारिक हो, तथा विद्वान वेदाचार्यों के मार्गदर्शन में संपन्न हो।**

परिवार की आवश्यकता और अनुष्ठान की प्रकृति के अनुसार Dharmaa, **श्राद्ध के लिए तीन प्रकार के विकल्प** उपलब्ध करा सकता है:

### **1. घर पर श्राद्ध — वेदाचार्य के मार्गदर्शन में**

परिवार अपने घर पर निर्धारित श्राद्ध-विधि स्वयं कर सकता है, जबकि एक योग्य वेदाचार्य उन्हें **लाइव, चरण-दर-चरण** संकल्प, तर्पण तथा अन्य आवश्यक विधियों का मार्गदर्शन प्रदान करते हैं।

### **2. वेदाचार्य द्वारा आपकी ओर से श्राद्ध**

यदि परिवार स्वयं श्राद्ध-विधि संपन्न करने में सक्षम या उपलब्ध नहीं है, तो हमारे वेदाचार्य आपकी ओर से निर्धारित श्राद्ध-विधि **किसी पारंपरिक मंदिर अथवा निर्धारित पवित्र स्थान** पर संपन्न कर सकते हैं। इसमें परिवार के नाम, गोत्र और संकल्प को विधिवत शामिल किया जाता है।

### **3. गया जी में श्राद्ध**

जो परिवार **गया जी में पिंडदान एवं श्राद्ध** करना चाहते हैं, उनके लिए Dharmaa अनुभवी स्थानीय पुरोहितों के माध्यम से पूरी प्रक्रिया को सुगम बनाने में सहायता कर सकता है।

उचित श्राद्ध-विधि, तिथि, संकल्प और आवश्यक व्यवस्थाओं के संबंध में मार्गदर्शन दिया जाता है — ताकि दूर देश या दूर शहर में रहने वाले परिवार भी इस महत्वपूर्ण परंपरा को **श्रद्धा और उचित विधि के साथ** निभा सकें।
