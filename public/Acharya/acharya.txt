Acharya | Sanskrit Scholar | Vedic Ritual & Karmakanda Specialist

Principal Vedacharya — Dharmaa Tribe | Online Pooja & Vedic Rituals

Dr. Ranganath Tripathi is a distinguished Sanskrit scholar, Acharya and Vedic ritual specialist with extensive academic and traditional training in Sanskrit literature, Vedic texts, Karmakanda, Paurohitya and Sanatan ritual traditions.

A scholar trained extensively in the Sanskrit tradition, Dr. Tripathi completed his education from the secondary level through Acharya entirely through the Sanskrit medium and holds multiple advanced qualifications across the Vedas, Sanskrit literature, Purāṇas, ritual traditions and pedagogy.

His scholarship combines traditional Shastric learning with rigorous academic research, enabling him to interpret and conduct Vedic rituals with both authenticity and intellectual depth.
