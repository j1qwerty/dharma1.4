Vaidik Shivam Pandey is a traditionally trained Sanskrit scholar and Vedic ritual practitioner from Shri Dham Ayodhya, with a strong foundation in Shukla Yajurveda, Sanskrit, Vedic Karmakanda, and Sanatan ritual traditions.

He received his traditional Shastri education in Ayodhya and completed his education up to Uttar-Madhyama through the Sanskrit medium. His academic and Vedic training combines formal Sanskrit education with traditional Vedic learning, enabling him to conduct sacred ceremonies in accordance with prescribed Shastric procedures and Sanatan Vedic traditions.

Academic & Vedic Qualifications

* Shukla Yajurveda — Madhyandina Shakha
* Shastri — Sampurnanand Sanskrit University, Ayodhya
* Uttar-Madhyama — Sanskrit-medium education
* B.A. in Sanskrit — Saket Mahavidyalaya, Ayodhya
* Intermediate — Shri Ram Veda Vidyalaya, Karsevakpuram, Ayodhya Dham

Areas of Expertise

Vaidik Shivam Pandey specialises in conducting religious ceremonies and Vedic Karmakanda according to Shastric procedures and Sanatan Vedic traditions, with particular experience in:

* Shodasha Samskaras — the sixteen traditional rites of passage
* Vedic Karmakanda & Puja Vidhi
* Rudrabhishek & Shiva Upasana
* Yajna, Havan & Vedic Anushthans
* Vedic Japa & Mantra Anushthans
* Vivaha Samskara & Traditional Religious Ceremonies
* Griha Pravesh & Vastu-related Pujas
* Navagraha Puja & Shanti Anushthans
* Shanti Path & Religious Anushthans
* Katha, Puja & Shastric Religious Procedures
* Traditional Religious Samskaras & Vedic Ceremonies

Acharya Rahul Dwivedi

Acharya | Sanskrit Scholar | Vedic Ritual & Karmakanda Specialist

He completed his Acharya education from Sampurnanand Sanskrit University, Varanasi, having pursued his entire education from the secondary level through Acharya in the Sanskrit medium. His diverse academic qualifications bring together traditional Sanskrit scholarship with specialised studies in Yoga and education.
